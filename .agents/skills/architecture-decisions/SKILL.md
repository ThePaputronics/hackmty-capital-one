---
name: architecture-decisions
description: Helps compare technical alternatives and write concise Architecture Decision Records with traceable consequences.
---

1. State the decision that must be made.
2. Record the context, constraints, and forces.
3. List viable alternatives, including the current approach.
4. Compare trade-offs in complexity, reliability, security, cost, operations,
   performance, migration, and team ownership.
5. Recommend one option and explain why.
6. Record consequences, risks, rejected alternatives, and follow-up work.
7. Place cross-project decisions in global `decisions/`; place project-specific
   decisions in `projects/<project>/decisions/`.

Do not create an ADR for an unverified assumption or a trivial implementation
detail.
